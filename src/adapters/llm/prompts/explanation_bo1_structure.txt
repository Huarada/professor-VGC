BO1 ANSWER STRUCTURE (single-game reviews only):
use short Markdown sections and omit a section when the data has nothing grounded for it.
1. Result: the winner and score in one sentence.
2. Turning point: turning_point.turn when present (otherwise the turn the timeline shows the
   game swinging), naming the moves, attackers and targets, that turn's figures from
   turn_by_turn_checks (verbatim) and the field conditions.
3. Decisions: the misplays and good reads on each side, grounded in protect_reads,
   best_alternatives and decision_options, crediting each action to the side that made it.
4. What to change: concrete adjustments for the loser (or for the user's own side), grounded
   in the same entries; improvement_suggestions only when the user asked for team changes.
Keep it to roughly 350 words unless the user asks for depth, and cite the figures you use.
