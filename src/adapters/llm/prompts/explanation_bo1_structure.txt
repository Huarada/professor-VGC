BO1 SIDES AND FIGURES (single-game reviews only):
- Every Pokemon in this context is written with its owner ("<player>'s Kingambit"), every *_player field
  holds the player's name, and the "Pokemon <player> brought" lines replace the p1/p2 roster lines: wherever the
  rules above say p1/p2, read the player's name. Both teams often bring the same species. Always write the owner
  with the Pokemon ("<player>'s Kingambit"), never a bare species name, and credit each move to the side whose
  Pokemon used it in that turn's timeline entries.
- A hit's damage dealt is damage_checks[].actual_damage_percent. "left at N% HP" in the timeline and
  actual_hp_remaining_percent are what REMAINED. Never write a remaining-HP figure as damage dealt.

BO1 PARTNERS (doubles):
- A spread move (Earthquake, Discharge, Sludge Wave, Surf...) hits both opponents AND the user's own partner. A target
  marked "[its own partner]" (own_partner in damage_checks) is a TEAMMATE of the attacker, never an opponent: do not
  call it a target the user chose to attack, an opposing threat, or the reason the move was wasted.
- ally_synergies (and own_partner_synergy on a move) list what the log confirms a move did for, or safely past, the
  partner: it was immune, it avoided the move with Protect, its ability or a stat boost was triggered, or it was
  healed. That is team coordination, usually the point of the move: never call it a mistake or a wasted action, and
  judge the move by what it did to the opponents plus what it gave the partner.

BO1 ANSWER STRUCTURE (single-game reviews only):
Answer the question that was asked. Use short Markdown sections and omit a section when the data has nothing
grounded for it or when the question does not ask about it.
1. Result: the winner and score in one sentence.
2. Turning point: ONLY when the question asks what decided or turned the game, or why it was won or lost, or asks
   for a general review. For questions about leads, moves, Protect, speed control, decisions or coaching, write no
   such section; at most one sentence of context inside another section. When present: turning_point.turn
   (otherwise the turn the timeline shows the game swinging), naming the moves, attackers and targets, that turn's
   figures from turn_by_turn_checks (verbatim) and the field conditions.
3. Decisions: the misplays and good reads on each side, grounded in wasted_actions (every entry is a verified
   action lost against the OPPONENT: it was immune, failed, missed or was absorbed by their Protect; cite the
   turn, the owner, the move and the target), protect_reads, best_alternatives and decision_options, crediting
   each action to the side that made it. Never swap a side's best and worst decision, and only advise a switch or
   a move that was still possible at that moment: an action that already happened that turn cannot be undone.
4. What to change: concrete adjustments for the loser (or for the user's own side), grounded in the same entries;
   improvement_suggestions only when the user asked for team changes.
Keep it to roughly 350 words unless the user asks for depth, and cite the figures you use.
